# Demo Script — Campus Opportunity Radar

> **90-second pitch + demo choreography. Practice this until it flows naturally.**

---

## Before the Demo

- ✅ Genie Space is open in browser with all 16 tables loaded
- ✅ Clear the conversation history (fresh start)
- ✅ Have this script visible on a second screen or printed
- ✅ Practice the timing — 90 seconds is tight

---

## The 90-Second Demo

### 🎬 0:00 – 0:15 | THE HOOK (Problem Statement)

> **Say this while showing the Genie Space:**
>
> *"Every university student faces the same problem: campus opportunities are everywhere — courses, clubs, projects, research, hackathons, placements — but they're all disconnected. A student who wants to become an AI Engineer doesn't know which club to join first, which project builds the right skills, or which workshop unlocks a research opportunity.*
>
> *What if a student could just ask... and get a personalized path?"*

---

### 🎬 0:15 – 0:30 | THE GOLDEN QUESTION (Type into Genie)

> **Type this question into the Genie Space:**

```
I'm Arjun Mehta, a 2nd-year CSE student. I know Java and SQL at an intermediate level. My goal is to become an AI Engineer. What campus opportunities should I pursue and in what order?
```

> **While Genie processes, say:**
>
> *"Genie is now reasoning across 8 connected tables — students, skills, projects, clubs, events, research, hackathons, and placements — all connected through a skill-based Opportunity Graph."*

---

### 🎬 0:30 – 0:55 | THE MAGIC MOMENT (Genie's Response)

> **Point out key elements of Genie's response:**
>
> *"Look at what Genie found — a step-by-step opportunity path:*
>
> *First, join the Backend API Project — Arjun already has Java and SQL, so he qualifies. This teaches him Python and REST APIs.*
>
> *Next, the AI/ML Club — now that he has Python, he can join. This introduces Machine Learning and Statistics.*
>
> *Then the Applied AI Workshop — hands-on TensorFlow, which is directly required by AI Engineer roles.*
>
> *Then Prof. Kumar's NLP Research — this deepens his ML skills and adds research experience.*
>
> *And after DataHack Hackathon for end-to-end project experience...*
>
> *He's ready for TechCorp's AI Engineer placement at 18 LPA."*
>
> *"Every recommendation explains WHY — which skill gap it fills and what it unlocks next."*

---

### 🎬 0:55 – 1:15 | THE "WHAT IF?" (Re-Planning)

> **Type this follow-up:**

```
What if Arjun only has 5 hours per week available?
```

> **When Genie responds, say:**
>
> *"Now watch — Genie re-plans the entire path. It drops the 8-hour research commitment, suggests lighter alternatives, and extends the timeline. Same goal, different constraints, completely different path.*
>
> *This is the power of reasoning over a graph — not just searching, but planning."*

---

### 🎬 1:15 – 1:30 | THE CLOSE (Why This Matters)

> *"Every student starts from a different place. Every student has different constraints. Campus Opportunity Radar gives each one a personalized, explainable path — powered by Genie reasoning across the campus Opportunity Graph.*
>
> *And it's not just for students — faculty can find research candidates, placement cells can track readiness, and administrators can identify campus-wide skill gaps.*
>
> *One graph. Every stakeholder. Powered by Genie."*

## Demo Disaster Recovery

| Problem | Recovery |
|---------|----------|
| Genie gives shallow answer | Say "Let me refine" and add: "Please reason step-by-step across all tables and explain why each opportunity fits" |
| Genie takes too long | Talk about the architecture while it processes: "Under the hood, Genie is generating SQL that joins 8+ tables..." |
| Genie errors | Switch to a backup question — "Let me show another capability" |
| Network issues | Have screenshots of expected output ready as backup |
